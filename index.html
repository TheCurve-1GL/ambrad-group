Projects

Recent project: 84 Wandsworth Bridge Road, Fulham

We converted a Victorian mixed-use building on Wandsworth Bridge Road into four self-contained flats: one three-bed, two two-beds and a one-bed. The work included a loft conversion, rear dormer, second-floor extension and side-return extension, in the Studdridge Street Conservation Area.

  • Planning: full permission granted in June 2023.
  • Finish: designed by an interior designer, with Schmidt kitchens and Siemens appliances throughout.
  • Outdoor space: the three-bed flat has a private 30 m² south-facing garden.
